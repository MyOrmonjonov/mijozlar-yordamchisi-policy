1. What the App does

Mijozlar Yordamchisi is a customer-assistant tool for the Instagram professional account of SHOX International Hospital. It helps the business read and answer direct messages and comments sent by customers, for example to answer questions about services, working hours and appointments.

2. Information we receive

When a person messages or comments on our Instagram professional account, we receive through the Instagram API (Meta Platform Data):

  • the person's Instagram-scoped ID and public profile name or username;
  • the content of the messages and comments they send to the account;
  • basic information about the connected Instagram professional account (ID, username, profile information).

We do not receive passwords. We do not collect data from people who have not contacted the account.

3. How we use information

  • to read incoming messages and comments and prepare or send replies on behalf of the business;
  • to keep conversation history so replies make sense in context;
  • to improve service quality and to keep the App secure.

We do not sell Platform Data, do not use it for advertising, and do not share it with data brokers.

4. Sharing

Data is shared only with service providers that host or process it for us (for example hosting and database providers), under confidentiality obligations, or when required by law. Medical or health details are not requested by the App; customers are asked to discuss sensitive health matters directly with the hospital.

5. Retention

Conversation data is kept only as long as needed to provide the service, and no longer than 12 months, after which it is deleted or anonymised. You can ask for earlier deletion at any time (see section 7).

6. Security

We protect data with access controls and encrypted connections (HTTPS). Access tokens are stored securely and only authorised staff can access conversation data.

7. Your rights and data deletion

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data. See our Data Deletion Instructions or email ormonjonovmuhammadyusf@gmail.com. We respond within 30 days.

8. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

9. Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
