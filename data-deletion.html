Data Deletion Instructions

Mijozlar Yordamchisi stores only the Instagram data you send to the SHOX International Hospital professional account (your Instagram-scoped ID, name/username, and message and comment content).

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to ormonjonovmuhammadyusf@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data".
  2. Include your Instagram username so we can find your records.
  3. We will delete all data linked to your account and confirm by email within 30 days.

Remove the App's access (account owners)

In Instagram go to Settings, then Website permissions, then Apps and Websites, find "Mijozlar Yordamchisi" and remove it. After removal we stop receiving new data from your account, and you may request deletion of stored data using the steps above.

See also our Privacy Policy.
